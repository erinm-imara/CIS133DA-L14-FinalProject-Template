Des Moines VA Medical Center

For local health services, you can find a variety of care at the Des Moines VA Medical Center. The VA provides health and wellbeing services for veterans in the Des Moines area.

Types of Services Offered

  • Primary care (southside location)
  • Cancer care
  • Diabetes care
  • Mental health care
  • Spinal cord injuries and disorders
  • Suicide prevention
  • And more
