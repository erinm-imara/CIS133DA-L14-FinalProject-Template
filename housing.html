Housing Resources for Veterans

If you or a veteran you know needs help finding housing, these local organizations in the Des Moines area can provide housing assistance, supportive services, and connections to additional resources.

Des Moines VA Community Resource & Referral Center (CRRC)

Address: 1223 Center Street, Suite 17, Des Moines, IA 50309

Phone: (515) 699-5637 (Main) or (877) 424-3838 (Emergency Hotline)

Website: Des Moines VA Community Resource & Referral Center (CRRC)

Details: Offers walk-in support, nurse evaluations, a day room with computers, laundry facilities, showers, and connections to HUD-VASH (HUD-Veterans Affairs Supportive Housing) vouchers and rapid rehousing.

Central Iowa Shelter & Services (CISS) Veteran Program

Address: 1420 Mulberry Street, Des Moines, IA 50309

Phone: (515) 284-5719

Website: Central Iowa Shelter & Services (CISS) Veteran Program

Details: Veteran Grand and Per Diem Program. Efficiency apartment, three meals per day, a clothing closet, food pantry, laundry access, and biweekly case management with the goal of permanent housing within 90 days.

Alley Landing

Address: 2715 Douglas Avenue, Des Moines, IA 50310

Website: Alley Landing

Details: Affordable rental apartments paired with onsite supportive services.
